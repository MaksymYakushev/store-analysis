# Store Sales Analysis

## Project Overview

This project demonstrates how data analysis and visualization can be used to evaluate retail sales performance, monitor key business metrics and support data-driven decision-making.

## 🏢 **Business Context**

Retail businesses generate large amounts of sales data, but without proper analysis it is difficult to understand what drives profitability and business growth. This project analyzes retail store performance across products, categories, customer segments, and regions. The objective is to uncover patterns in sales, profit, and discount strategies in order to identify both growth opportunities and operational issues.

**The analysis focuses on:**

- sales and profit performance  
- discount impact on profitability 
- regional and state-level trends 
- customer segment behavior 
- product-level performance 

## 💼 **Business Value**

The dashboard transforms raw transactional data into actionable business insights that support data-driven decision-making.

**The analysis helps:**

- identify the most and least profitable products 
- detect unprofitable discount strategies 
- compare regional business performance 
- track important KPIs over time 
- improve decisions related to pricing, sales strategy, and inventory management 

Interactive Tableau visualizations allow stakeholders to quickly monitor business performance and discover areas that require attention or optimization. 

## 📈 **Dashboard Features**


* **Interactive Filters** – Users can dynamically filter all dashboard views by state, product category, customer segment, and specific order date quarters.
* **Summary KPIs** – Displays core business metrics at a glance, including Total Sales, Total Profit, Profit Margin, Total Orders, Total Customers, and Mean Order Value.
* **Sales & Profit Trends** – Timeline visualizations with trendlines that track sales dynamics and profitability fluctuations over years and quarters.
* **Product Performance** – Deep dive into product categories and sub-categories, featuring Top-5 lists of the most profitable and most unprofitable products.
* **Discount Impact Analysis** – Evaluation of how discount levels (from 0% to 80%+) affect profit margins, highlighting areas where high discounts cause financial losses.
* **Customer Breakdown** – Insights into customer segments (Consumer vs. Corporate) and behavior, identifying top-performing accounts alongside underperforming ones.
* **Geographical Insights** – An interactive map and regional charts mapping out total sales and profit distribution across different states and cities.
